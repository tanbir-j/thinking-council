Close read this one. Same paper, the section on why forecasts drift. Reader is a finance director.

A forecast is only as current as its slowest input. In most groups that input is headcount, because hiring decisions are made in the business and reach Finance through a monthly extract. A role approved on the third of the month appears in the forecast five weeks later, by which time the salary is already being paid. Checking the date of the newest headcount record against the forecast date shows the size of the gap in a minute.
