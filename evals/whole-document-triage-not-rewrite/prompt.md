Close read the whole of this short piece for me. Reader is a CFO.

1. Month-end takes too long in most finance teams.

2. The usual response is to buy a close management tool. Tools help with tracking tasks.

3. Close is slow in most teams, and leaders are frustrated by how long it takes.

4. The delay usually sits in three or four reconciliations that wait on another team.

5. Those reconciliations wait because the other team's deadline is set by its own calendar.

6. A tool that tracks tasks will show the wait and will not shorten it.

7. Aligning the two calendars removes the wait.

8. Leaders should think carefully about change.
