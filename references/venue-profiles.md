# Venue Profiles

Identify the venue before reviewing. If the user has not stated it, infer it from length, register, and structure, and state the inference. Every recommendation must fit the venue's container: where a word budget applies, pair every Develop with a Cut offset of comparable size.

## Trade publication feature article

- Budget: 700 to 1,200 words. Treat 1,200 as a wall, not a target.
- Standard: publishable thought leadership for practitioners and leaders in the field, with an intelligent lay reader as the clarity floor.
- An editorial gatekeeper usually applies. Where the author profile says the venue uses an AI-detection gate, run the sweep at Final Gate.
- Structure: each section owns one idea. A 1,000-word article carries one central claim and at most three supporting moves.

## Series part (practitioner series)

- Budget: roughly 1,000 words per part.
- Each part must stand alone for a first-time reader and still advance the series argument. Test both.
- Check consistency of any series-level IP (frameworks, ladders, doctrines) with earlier parts. Contradiction across parts is a material finding.
- Cross-references to other parts should orient, not depend: a reader who missed part two must still follow part four.

## White paper chapter or section

- No fixed word budget. The discipline is structural: one irrefutable idea per section, MECE boundaries between sections, SCR at paragraph level.
- Reviewer additions and co-author material must be checked for voice consistency with the surrounding prose.
- Figures are part of the argument. Check that figure content matches body text, and remember that text rendered inside figures escapes a standard editorial pass; flag it separately.

## Professional network post

- The opening line carries full responsibility for earning the "see more" click. Judge it as its own artefact.
- Hashtags belong in the post body, not the comments.
- Article links go in the first comment, not the post body.
- Budget: the argument must land within the pre-fold text plus a few short paragraphs. A post is not a compressed article; it is one idea with one hook and one consequence.

## Carousel

- Format: PDF at 1080 x 1350 pixels portrait, typically around 12 slides.
- One idea per slide. A slide that needs a paragraph is two slides or none.
- The first slide does the opening line's job; the last slide states the reader's next action.
- Review the slide sequence as a narrative thread, exactly as for an article: opening, development, resolution.

## Unknown or hybrid venue

State that the venue is unidentified, review against the closest profile, and flag which findings would change if the venue turns out to be different.
