# Author Profile (template)

This file tells the council who the author is, what they want to be known for, and how they work. Fill it in, or keep a filled copy at `references/private/author-profile.md`. The private copy takes precedence and is excluded from version control, so positioning notes never reach a public repository.

Until a profile is filled in: infer the author's territory and audience from the work itself, ask once if a verdict depends on a missing answer, and mark every personal-brand and positioning verdict as provisional.

Treat a filled profile as a starting charter. When the author's published work is available, search it first and base the body-of-work judgement on the work itself. If the two conflict, trust the work and flag the conflict so the profile can be updated.

## Brand-strengthening signals

A piece should normally do at least two of these. Edit the list to fit the author.

1. Introduce a distinctive way to think about a problem in the core territory.
2. Convert a broad idea into a practical method.
3. Connect the core territory with an adjacent one.
4. Challenge a comfortable assumption held by the primary audience.
5. Provide language, frameworks, or analogies readers may reuse.
6. Demonstrate lessons earned through delivery, with the detail that proves it.

## Brand risks

Flag work that:

- drifts into generic commentary without an anchor in the core territory
- repeats familiar advice without a new mechanism or perspective
- claims more than the evidence or experience earns
- uses structure without practitioner substance
- uses analogy or popular culture as decoration
- chases a topical subject that does not strengthen the wider body of work

## Personal-brand test

Assess four questions and give the verdict defined in the article profile.

- **Territory.** Does the work reinforce or credibly extend the core territory?
- **Authority.** Does it show both structured thinking and practical experience?
- **Recognition.** Does it contain an idea, phrase, framework, or analogy readers could associate with this author?
- **Cumulative value.** Does it advance the body of work or connect existing themes?

## Body-of-work research

When the author's earlier work is available, search it. Compare the new piece for repetition without advancement, development of an earlier idea, links between themes, contradiction that should be explained, and a potential signature framework. If the body of work is incomplete, say the personal-brand verdict is provisional.
