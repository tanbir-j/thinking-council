# Output Formats

These are defaults, not mandates. Adapt headings, add sections the material demands, and collapse any section without a material finding to a single line: "Nothing material." A review is judged by its findings, not its length.

Four elements are mandatory in every review mode and may never be collapsed: the verdict, the findings, the strongest objection, and the next move. Everything else is adaptive.

Open Field results appear in every Council mode as "Angles carried forward": each angle adopted, adapted, or killed with the reason. None may be dropped silently.

## Single counsel

One seat, in its own method, with no chamber apparatus. Shape:

1. The seat and its method in one line
2. The verdict
3. Findings in that seat's terms (three to five)
4. The strongest objection from that seat's perspective
5. Next move

If the seat's method cannot reach a finding the user needs, say so and name the seat that would.

## Open Field

Prose, without council vocabulary or headings until the close. Shape:

1. The problem restated more precisely than the user stated it, and any challenge to the objective, audience, or deliverable
2. The thinking: hypotheses, tensions, what is not being said, an adjacent-domain analogy where one earns its place
3. The ten-out-of-ten version of the eventual output, in a paragraph
4. Angles not yet considered, ranked, at least three, one outside the user's usual territory
5. The next move: the one thing to find out or decide

No scores. No verdict apparatus beyond a clear view.

## Close Read

Paragraph-level review, one block at a time. The diagnostic shape, the targeted rewrite rules, and the reconcile steps are in [close-read.md](close-read.md), sections 8 to 10. Close Read is exempt from the mandatory four: its verdict is Keep, Fix, Cut or merge, or Query first, and a Keep takes one line.

## Council Glance

Use this compact structure:

### Mode selected

State the artefact stage, intended outcome, audience, and why Council Glance applies.

### Immediate verdict

Give the direct judgement in one short paragraph.

### What is working

Identify the strongest idea or asset to protect.

### Angles carried forward

From Open Field: adopted, adapted, or killed with the reason. One line each.

### Where it falls short

Give three to five findings. For each:

- finding
- why it matters
- practical improvement

### Thinking balance

State the next required balance of Compression, Expansion, Reflection, and Execution. Name only the selected philosophers.

### Positioning verdicts

Give editorial verdicts for innovation, thought leadership, and personal brand, plus the credibility verdict where the audience includes an executive or client. State what would raise each by one level.

### Next move

Name the single highest-value next action.

## Full Council

### 1. Mode and assignment

State operating mode, article stage, audience, objective, desired reader response, and any inferred assumptions.

### 2. Overall verdict

Explain how close the article is to achieving its objective. Identify the main constraint.

### 3. What must be protected

Name the distinctive idea, passage, analogy, voice, evidence, or structure that revisions must preserve.

### 3a. Angles carried forward

From Open Field: adopted, adapted, or killed with the reason.

### 4. Harness Team assessment

Give one integrated assessment across:

- strategic context and problem definition
- evidence and inference
- positioning and persuasion
- audience, people, and change
- practical clarity and usability

### 5. Scorecard (article or consultancy, per the profile)

Score the eight mature-profile dimensions from 1 to 5 with a brief rationale. Do not average.

### 6. Structural diagnosis

Cover:

- thesis
- narrative thread in one sentence
- coherence
- selective MECE
- section sequence
- opening, transitions, and conclusion
- plain-language barriers

### 6a. Meaning gate

Up to five blocks where the reasoning fails, from the triage in [close-read.md](close-read.md) section 14. For each: the block, its intended job, the missing link, distinction, or consequence, the likely operation, what to protect, any claim risk, and the net word cost. Collapse to "Nothing material" when the reasoning holds. Never rewrite the manuscript here; offer a Close Read on the listed blocks.

### 7. Thinking-mode diagnosis and appointed counsel

State the current and required mode balance. Include only counsel that adds a distinct finding. Explain disagreements where they reveal a real choice.

### 8. The strongest objection

Mandatory in every mode. State the single strongest objection a hostile, informed reader or the most dangerous executive in the room would raise, in their terms, and assess whether the work survives it. If the council cannot construct a serious objection, say so and treat that as evidence the claim is too safe to matter.

### 9. Priority improvement agenda

Rank three to five actions using Reconsider, Develop, Restructure, Clarify, Cut, and Protect. State impact and suggested sequence. Where the venue carries a word budget, pair every Develop with a Cut offset.

### 10. Verdicts

Include the credibility verdict from [executive-credibility.md](executive-credibility.md) whenever the audience includes an executive, board, owner, or client decision-maker.

Give separate verdicts for:

- publication readiness
- innovation
- thought leadership
- personal brand

For each positioning verdict, state what would raise it by one level.

## Boardroom Rehearsal

The CFO in the room seat leads, instantiated as each named executive. Shape:

1. The executives simulated, with the situation assumed for each and its source (project knowledge, past conversation, or inference)
2. For each executive: the 30-second read; where they stop; the unanticipated question; what they conclude about the author; what they need to say upward and whether the artefact gives it to them
3. Alliance map: what is in it for each, what they fear, whose idea the decision can become
4. Register check and expertise-signals audit, from [executive-credibility.md](executive-credibility.md)
5. The strongest objection, in the most dangerous executive's own words
6. Credibility verdict, with the single change that raises it one level
7. Fixes in order, cheapest decisive first
8. Next move

## Final Gate

Two variants. Both end in a readiness decision and neither may be collapsed. Both run the meaning gate from [close-read.md](close-read.md) section 14 before any style check and report it when it finds something material.

### Client variant

1. Audit partner pass: material assertions listed with evidence status; anything unevidenced named
2. CFO in the room 30-second read and the unanticipated question
3. Register check and expertise-signals audit
4. What the sponsor has not yet seen that they must see first
5. The strongest objection
6. Credibility verdict
7. Go, go with named fixes, or hold, with the fixes ranked

### Publication variant (formerly Publication Tribunal)

Use the Full Council structure, then add:

### Evidence-backed originality review

For each material comparable source, state:

- the overlapping claim, framework, language, or analogy
- the degree of overlap
- what remains distinctive in the manuscript
- whether attribution, reframing, or a narrower claim is needed

Synthesize the research into one of:

- original idea
- original synthesis
- original application
- fresh communication
- material prior art

State confidence and limitations. Cite all material external claims.

### AI-signature sweep

Run this where the author profile says the venue applies an AI-detection gate, and on request elsewhere.

1. Find the author's style guide (named in the author profile) and apply its AI-signature sections as the authoritative pattern list. Cite section numbers rather than restating rules.
2. If the guide is unreachable, fall back to this minimum pattern set: em dashes; the "X is not Y, it is Z" negation pattern; same-scaffold parallel structure across consecutive sentences; "The [noun]" opener immediately after a short sentence; manufactured enthusiasm and hype intensifiers; descriptive triads inside prose.
3. Report each flagged passage with its location and the pattern it matches. Flag candidates; the author decides.
4. State a directional risk judgement only. Never claim to predict the result of any AI-detection tool, and never present a clean sweep as a guarantee of passing a gate.

### Body-of-work fit

When project knowledge or uploaded work is available, search it and state whether this manuscript repeats, develops, connects, contradicts, extends, or creates signature territory.

### Final publication decision

Give one clear decision and the minimum changes required before publication.

## Comparison review

When comparing versions, use:

1. Shared objective
2. Winner by dimension
3. Trade-offs
4. Material worth preserving from each
5. Hybrid recommendation, if stronger
6. Final choice

Do not declare a winner without explaining the cost of the choice.

## Revision review

Retrieve the previous draft or council report before scoring: search past conversations if it is not supplied.

Use:

1. Issues resolved
2. Issues still open
3. New problems introduced
4. Distinctive material preserved or lost
5. Updated scores and verdicts only where changed, with any lowered score stated plainly and explained
6. Publication decision

Scores are re-earned, not inherited. A revision review that can only move scores upward is miscalibrated.
